# Aether Vision

> *"Your home, managed. Your mind, free."*

## What is Aether?

Aether is a **Home Concierge** — a system that lifts the mental burden of remembering what needs to be done around your home, when, and how urgently.

It is **not** a personal assistant, task manager, or life OS. It does one thing well: home management.

## Core Philosophy

### 1. Offload, Don't Add
Aether reduces mental load. It never creates more. If something is "in Aether," you don't need to think about it until Aether surfaces it.

### 2. Flexible, Not Strict
Life happens. Intervals are guidelines, not deadlines. A 2-day grace period is built in. Nothing nags.

### 3. Adaptive
Aether learns your patterns. If you consistently vacuum every 5 days instead of 7, it will notice and ask if you want to adjust. It always confirms before changing.

### 4. Friendly, Not Naggy
Low-pressure information delivery. You check Aether when you want to. Morning briefings are friendly suggestions, not demands.

### 5. Classy Gamification
Satisfying completions, streaks, and visual flair — but elegant. Think New York doorman, not mobile game. No confetti, no cartoon rewards.

### 6. Room-First Thinking
Your mental model of home is room-based. You walk into a room and it "feels off." Aether matches this: rooms are the primary organizing unit.

## Core Use Cases

### "I have 30 minutes. What should I do?"
Aether returns a prioritized list of chores that fit your time budget and will make the biggest difference.

### "When did I last vacuum the bedroom?"
Aether knows. You don't have to remember.

### "My parents are visiting this weekend."
Open the "Visitors" checklist. See exactly what needs attention before they arrive.

### "Is the living room okay?"
Check the room's freshness score. Green means fresh. Yellow means attention needed soon. Red means overdue.

## What Aether Is NOT

- **Not a task manager**: Use Todoist, Things, or whatever you prefer for general tasks
- **Not a calendar**: It doesn't schedule your day
- **Not an assistant**: It doesn't answer questions or chat
- **Not for daily tasks**: Making the bed, laundry, taking out garbage — too frequent and variable
- **Not for projects**: Home renovations are out of scope (for now)

## Target User

Someone who:
- Lives alone or manages their own space
- Has ADHD or simply wants less mental load
- Prefers lists and structure over "winging it"
- Wants help with the recurring maintenance that's easy to forget
- Values their home feeling clean and managed

## Success Metrics

Aether is successful when:
1. You stop randomly remembering chores at 2am
2. Rare tasks (oil floors, smoke detector batteries) actually get done
3. Your home feels consistently maintained, not crisis-cleaned
4. You can say "I have 20 minutes" and immediately know what to do
